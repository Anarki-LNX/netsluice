---
question: Which functions are not finished?
lang: en
category: basics
order: 6
---
- **Inline ("sluice") mode and listening mode:** not implemented.
- **Changing the fixed address from the interface:** not implemented. The interface shows the address but does not change it yet.
- **USB-C as a data port:** does not work. It still powers the device.
- **Other board revisions:** untested.
- **A WireGuard tunnel** to use the filter away from home is an idea for later. It is not planned and not built.
- **Tests for the interface** do not exist yet.
