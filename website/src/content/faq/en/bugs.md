---
question: How do I report a bug?
lang: en
category: project
order: 50
---
Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Anarki-LNX/netsluice/issues). Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened, with the version from Settings, Updates. For a security problem, leave out the details in public and write to hallo@loopout.tech. See [Contributing](/en/contributing/) and [Security](/en/security/).
