---
question: How do I install it?
lang: en
category: install
order: 10
---
Download the ready-made image, check its checksum, write it to a microSD card (8 GB or more) with balenaEtcher or the Raspberry Pi Imager, put the card into the Mini, connect it to your router and power it up. After a few minutes open `http://netsluice.local/,` choose the password and enter the device's address as the DNS server in your router.

The [getting started page](/en/getting-started/) and the [installation guide](/en/docs/install-image/) go through it step by step. You can also [build it yourself](/en/docs/build-from-source/).
