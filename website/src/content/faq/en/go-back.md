---
question: Can I go back to the original software?
lang: en
category: install
order: 12
---
Yes. While NetSluice runs from the card, remove the card.

If you installed NetSluice to the internal storage, you need the backup you made first. Start the device from a NetSluice card that holds it, and choose "Put the backup back" under Settings, Back up and install. The backup is checked before anything is written, and it only goes back to the storage it came from. Without a backup the original system cannot be brought back. See [Back up and install](/en/docs/backup-and-install/).
