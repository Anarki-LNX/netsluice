---
question: How do I switch the filter off for a moment?
lang: en
category: use
order: 23
---
Click "Filter on" in the top bar of the interface. The filter pauses for ten minutes and then comes back by itself, unless you choose "Keep it off". Or press the button on the device: filter off for ten minutes, a second press switches it back on. The red LED blinks three times as a receipt.
