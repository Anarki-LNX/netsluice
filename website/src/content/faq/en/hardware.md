lang: en

The Deeper Connect Mini. NetSluice was developed and tested on one device with the board marking `mini_v21_20210521` (Rockchip RK3328, 2 GB RAM, 8 GB eMMC). Other revisions of the Mini are untested. If you try one, please [report what you find](/en/contributing/), including failures. See the [hardware page](/en/hardware/).
