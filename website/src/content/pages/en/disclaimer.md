## No warranty

NetSluice is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind, as the [MIT licence](/en/license/) says. You are modifying a network device at your own risk. A filter that fails takes the name lookups of your network with it.

## Before you change anything

- **Running from the card** changes nothing on the device. Remove the card and the original system starts again.
- **Installing to the internal storage erases the original system**, including any wallet on it. Make the backup first and download it. Make sure you have your wallet's seed phrase or keystore.
- **Putting a backup back** only works with a valid backup of that same device.
- **Your board may differ.** NetSluice was tested on one board with the marking `mini_v21_20210521`. Other revisions are untested. The Pico, Nano and Air are not supported.
- **Do not expose the interface** to the internet. See [Security](/en/security/).
