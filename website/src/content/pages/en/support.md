## Questions and problems

- **GitHub issues** are the place for bugs, questions and ideas: [github.com/Anarki-LNX/netsluice/issues](https://github.com/Anarki-LNX/netsluice/issues). Others can find the answer there later.
- **E-mail** for anything that does not belong in public, such as a security problem: **hallo@loopout.tech**.

There is no forum, chat or phone support.

Before you ask, the [FAQ](/en/faq/) and the [documentation](/en/docs/) may already have the answer.

## Support the project

NetSluice is free and open source under the MIT licence. There are no paid features, and a donation does not unlock anything.

If it is useful to you and you want to give something back, a donation via PayPal helps keep it going: <a href="https://www.paypal.me/HenryMeinekat" rel="noopener">donate via PayPal</a>.

Money is only one way. Reports from other boards, bug reports and pull requests help just as much. See [Contributing](/en/contributing/).
